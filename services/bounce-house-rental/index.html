Common questions

Bounce house FAQ

How far in advance should I book a bounce house?

We recommend booking at least one to two weeks ahead, especially for weekend dates during spring and summer. Same-week availability is sometimes possible for rentals only, but booking early gives you the best selection. For full event planning with a rental, four or more weeks is ideal.

Do you deliver to parks and public venues?

Yes. We deliver to parks, school grounds, community centers, and private properties throughout Champaign County. Some public venues require proof of insurance, which we can provide. We also work with corporate event venues regularly.

What is your weather policy?

Safety is the priority. We do not set up inflatables in high winds, lightning, or heavy rain. If weather forces a cancellation on the day of your event, we will work with you to reschedule.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a deposit is required to hold your date. Contact us for current deposit amounts and payment options. Kei Events has been serving Champaign County since 2020 and always provides transparent pricing upfront.

What size space do I need for setup?

Space requirements vary by unit. Most standard castle combos need at least a 15x15 foot flat area. Obstacle courses require more length. When you request a quote, let us know your venue dimensions and we will match you with the right unit.
